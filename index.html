km 5.0 · The Safayat's World Fund

I was given a scholarship. Now I give them.

Türkiye Bursları, Erasmus+ and the Yunus & Youth Fellowship opened the world to me. The Safayat's World Fund backs unemployed founders and outstanding engineers who use AI to modernise civil, transport, water and environmental engineering.

Opening first

Seed funding for unemployed people to start and run their own business, built on AI and modern engineering.
